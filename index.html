[ 07 // PROTOCOL FAQ ]

Frequently Asked Inquiries

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) lets AI answer questions using your own documents and data instead of just its training data. You need it if you want accurate, private, domain-specific responses grounded in your business knowledge.

Chatbots reply. Agents act. AI agents can plan multi-step workflows, call APIs, update your CRM, send emails, and execute tasks autonomously: not just conversation.

A focused AI chatbot or RAG system typically spans 3 to 5 weeks. Agent and automation workflows take 4 to 6 weeks. Full web or 3D web systems with AI integration run 4 to 8 weeks depending on scope.

Our AI stack centers on LLMs (OpenAI, Anthropic, open-source models), vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant), agent frameworks (LangChain, AutoGen), and modern web delivery (React, Node, Three.js for 3D interfaces).

We work on fixed-scope project milestones (typically 50% upfront, 25% at prototype approval, 25% on final deployment) or monthly advisory retainers for fast-moving startups and venture funds.
